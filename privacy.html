Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

BillNoty is a bill reminder and payment-status tracking application. This policy describes how the current version of BillNoty handles information.

1. Information you enter

You may enter bill names, amounts, due dates, payment statuses, recurrence selections, and optional notes. This information is stored in the application's local storage on your device.

2. Data collection and accounts

BillNoty does not require an account or sign-in. The current app does not send your saved bill records to a developer-operated server and does not provide cloud account synchronization.

3. Notifications

With your permission, BillNoty schedules local reminders on your device. Notification content can include a bill name, amount, and due date. Notifications may appear on your lock screen depending on your device settings. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

4. Advertising and analytics

The current version does not include advertising SDKs or developer-integrated analytics services.

5. Payments

BillNoty does not connect to bank accounts, transfer money, or make payments. “Ready for AutoPay” indicates a tracking status only and does not activate automatic payments.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your bill records remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app's local data. You can delete individual bills in BillNoty. Device backups may retain copies according to your device and backup settings.

7. Third-party software and platform services

BillNoty is built using Expo and React Native software components and uses iOS notification services. Apple and other platform providers may process technical information under their own policies. The current app code does not implement third-party advertising, analytics, or bill-data upload features.

8. Children's privacy

BillNoty is a general-purpose bill reminder app and is not specifically directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if BillNoty's features or information practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email billnoty.support@gmail.com.
