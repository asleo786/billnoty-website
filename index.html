Frequently asked questions

How do I add a bill?

Open BillNoty, select Add, enter the bill name, amount, and due date, then tap Save Bill.

How do I receive reminders?

Allow notifications for BillNoty in your iPhone settings. The app schedules local reminders before, on, and after a bill's due date.

How do I mark a bill as paid?

Open a bill's payment alert and select Already Paid. You can also choose Not Paid Yet or Ready for AutoPay.

Does BillNoty pay my bills?

No. BillNoty tracks payment status and reminds you about bills. It does not connect to banks or initiate payments. “Ready for AutoPay” is a tracking status only.

Where are my bills stored?

Bill records are saved in the app's local storage on your device. BillNoty does not offer account-based cloud synchronization in this version.

How do I delete a bill?

Open the Bills screen, select Delete next to the bill, and confirm.
